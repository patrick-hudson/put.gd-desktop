## Refresh, save, reset, async, and diagnostics

`refresh()` remains an intentional accepted-document reset: resets profile/raw backing state, revisions, dirty/conflict flags, and repopulates only built pages. `populate()` and `refreshDiagnostics()` must tolerate absent Capture/Shortcuts/Preferences. Continue excluding all three credential inputs from dirty/checkpoint data. Keep reset gating and callbacks: pending reset disables editing and emits `request_awaiting_acceptance`; success refreshes deferred backing state before close/reload completion; failure preserves dirty controls/backing state and credentials remain cleared. Keep `configurationChanged()` preserving dirty drafts on valid or invalid external changes, and refreshing clean drafts, without materializing pages.

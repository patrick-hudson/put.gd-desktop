## #120: the locked System audio box

Cause: the bar's stylesheet (`src/record/Overlays.cpp`) listed
`QCheckBox::indicator:disabled` after `:checked` with the same specificity,
and the checked indicator was only a fill, so a disabled checked box was
painted exactly like an unchecked one. From Record on the box is disabled,
so it looked empty on every desktop. The bar's config was not the cause:
before and during recording the bar and the pipeline both read the session's
`config_`, and the pipeline refuses to start without an audio encoder when
audio is on. The issue's frame (`wayland-av-sync/f44-gnome50-wayland-portal-window-recording-0500748.png`)
is the before picture.

Fix: the checked indicator carries the redesign theme's tick
(`src/ui/redesign-icons/check.png`, compiled in through `src/record/record.qrc`),
a `:checked:disabled` rule keeps a muted fill, and while recording the box's
tooltip says whether system audio is being recorded.

### Suites

- `bar_placement_contract` and `bar_placement_single_monitor_contract`
  gain `lockedAudioBoxShowsTheRecordedSound`: the real bar on the offscreen
  platform, rendered in the ready, recording, paused and stopping states with
  sound on and off. Passed on d69. Mutation check on d69: without the
  `:checked:disabled` rule it fails (`recording: mean 29.5833 vs 31`, the two
  indicators indistinguishable).
- The full suite passed on f44 (80/80, Fedora 44, Qt 6.11.2).

## #119: "Error parsing Opus packet header"

The WebM exports are fine. The message comes from FFmpeg 8.0's Opus
**parser** in the demuxer: with `PARSER_FLAG_COMPLETE_FRAMES` it parses the
empty flush buffer at EOF. It came in with FFmpeg 37155d68ec (January 2025),
was fixed in 8.1 by
[618fc15e65](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/618fc15e65f57c9ce25d4562f4b516129815608c)
(FFmpeg #20954), and was not backported to `release/8.0`.

| FFmpeg | Host | Our export (`putgd-record --export` of a d69 lab clip) | `ffmpeg` CLI `-c:a libopus` reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7.1.5 (Debian 13) | d69 | clean | `.webm` clean |
| 8.0.1 (Ubuntu 26.04) | k72 | one error at EOF | `.webm` and `.mka`: one error at EOF each, also for a length with no end padding; `.ogg` clean |
| 8.1.3 (Fedora 44) | f44 | clean | `.webm` clean |

The decoded PCM of our export is byte-identical on k72 and d69 (3,201,792
bytes, 800,448 samples). The "extra empty field" in `ffprobe` is the last
packet's `Skip Samples` side data, Matroska `DiscardPadding`, the normal Opus
end trim (840 of 960 samples in that file). The AppImage bundles FFmpeg
7.1.5 (`packaging/appimage/tools.lock.json`), which never had the regression.
Distro FFmpeg 8.0.x (Ubuntu 26.04's 8.0.1) still prints the message for any
WebM or Matroska Opus file until the distro takes 8.1 or the fix; that is
outside put.gd.

### Suites

- `export_av_sync_contract` gains `webmAudioDecodesEveryPacket`: the marker
  clip exported to WebM, untrimmed and trimmed, opened with
  `AVFMT_FLAG_NOPARSE` so the decoder sees every packet as written (the parser
  would drop a broken one), decoded with the native `opus` and the `libopus`
  decoders; any rejected packet or decoder-logged error fails, and the decoded
  length must be within one 20 ms frame of the export. Passed on d69 (FFmpeg
  7.1.5), on k72 (8.0.1, 13/13 including the #86 rows) and in CI (Ubuntu
  24.04 and 26.04).
- Mutation check on d69: with the last Opus packet overwritten in
  `ClipExporter`, all four rows fail (`packet 201 at pts 3994 rejected`).
  Without `AVFMT_FLAG_NOPARSE` they had passed.
